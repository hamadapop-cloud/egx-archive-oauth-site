EGX Archive

A privately operated tool to collect eligible, publicly accessible Egyptian stock market source files and preserve verified archive copies in the account owner's Google Drive.

For personal use

What the application does

  • Collects eligible publicly accessible source files and checks archive metadata.
  • Stores archived originals, manifests, state and limited diagnostic information in a designated folder in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Uses an automated GitHub Actions workflow and rclone to communicate with Google Drive.

Important: The Google OAuth connection currently requests broad Google Drive permission, even though the intended automated operation is restricted to the designated archive location. Review the privacy policy before granting access.

Personal, independent project

This application is for its owner's personal data archiving and is not offered as a public data service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Egyptian Exchange (EGX), Google or GitHub.
